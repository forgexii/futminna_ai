# User Guide — FUTMINNA AI

## Getting started

1. Go to **Create your free account** and sign up with your FUTMINNA email.
2. You'll land on your dashboard, which shows your trust score, quick actions, and (once you've used the platform a little) personalized recommendations.

## Buying and selling on the marketplace

- **To sell**: click **Create listing** from the sidebar (or dashboard quick action). Fill in a title, description, price, category, and condition, then publish. Be specific and honest about condition — it directly affects your trust score over time through ratings.
- **To buy**: browse or search the marketplace, open a listing, and click **Buy now**. For items over ₦5,000, your payment is held until you confirm you've received the item — go to **My orders** and click **Confirm receipt** once you have it. If something's wrong, click **Dispute** instead.
- **Favorites**: click the heart icon on any listing to save it for later; find your saved items from your favorites list.
- **Reporting**: if a listing looks wrong or a seller seems suspicious, click **Report this listing** on the listing page.

## Booking a tutor

1. Go to **Find a tutor** and search by course code (e.g. `MAT201`) or keyword.
2. Open a tutor's profile to see their rate, availability, expertise, and ratings from other students.
3. Click **Book a session**, choose a course code, date/time, and duration, then confirm.
4. Pay to complete the booking request — the tutor will accept or decline once payment clears.
5. After your session, the tutor marks it complete; you'll then be able to leave a rating.

## Becoming a tutor

1. From your dashboard or the tutors page, click **Become a tutor** / **Apply now**.
2. Add a headline, the course codes you can teach, your hourly rate, and a short bio.
3. An administrator reviews your application — you'll get a notification once it's approved (or if it needs changes).
4. Once approved, manage incoming bookings from **Bookings** and track your payouts from **Earnings**.

## Understanding your trust score

Your trust score (0-100) is shown on your profile and is built from:

- **Verification** (up to 20 points) — having a verified FUTMINNA account.
- **Completed transactions** (up to 30 points) — successful sales, purchases, and tutoring sessions.
- **Average rating** (up to 35 points) — feedback from people you've transacted with.
- **Responsiveness** (up to 10 points) — currently a baseline value; will reflect real message response time as messaging is added.
- **Disputes** (up to -25 points) — unresolved disputes lower your score.

You can see your full breakdown under **Settings**.

## The campus assistant

Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner from anywhere in the app. Ask about registration deadlines, the academic calendar, marketplace policies, or how any part of the platform works. It only answers from a curated knowledge base, and will say so if it doesn't have an answer rather than guessing — for anything sensitive (exact dates, fees), always double-check with the relevant university office.

## Notifications

Bell icon in the sidebar / **Notifications** page shows booking updates, payment confirmations, verification status changes, and moderation outcomes. Click any unread notification to mark it as read.
